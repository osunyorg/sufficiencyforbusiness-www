Join us at the Annual Conference of the European Academy of Management on "Rising Above – Embracing Resilience, Reimagining History, and Rethinking Management for a Sustainable Future", hosted by Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam

text: >-

Submit your contributions to track 01_14 by January 12th at https://conferences.euram.academy/2027conference/call-for-papers/ (external link)

This track calls for communications that contribute to the growing research field of sufficiency and its relations with post-growth in business. Key questions include, but are not limited to: How can sufficiency and post-growth as new business paradigms be scaled to established companies? How do sufficiency and post-growth challenge and inspire alternative notions of performance and impact? How can sufficiency- and post-growth-oriented business models be made desirable, feasible and viable, and with what management tools, practices, and indicators? What is the role of economic growth in sufficiency-oriented businesses? What systemic changes, including in consumption behaviours and policy orientations, are needed to support sufficiency and post-growth in business?

More info on the call for submissions are available at: https://conferences.euram.academy/2027conference/download/t01_-14-sufficiency-and-post-growth-as-new-business-paradigms/ (external link) 

notes: >-

The track is hosted by Benjamin Combes, Maike Gossen, Lotte Levelt, and Laura Niessen
